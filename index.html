ABOUT TEACH TO LEARN

PROMOTING POSITIVE YOUTH DEVELOPMENT THROUGH MENTORSHIP

Connected with Premier Higher Education Institutions Since 2015

The vibrant 'Teach to Learn' team at IIT Madras aims to promote positive youth development using the mentoring approach. Our overarching goal is to provide structured mentoring to rural school students using well-researched programs.

What do we do? We build mentoring models using the peer-teaching peer-learning mechanism. Our models aim to:

  • Develop scientific temper and make hands-on learning part of rural educational experience.
  • Give rural youth exposure and access to state-of-the-art technology (3D printing & device engineering).
  • Motivate graduate and undergraduate students (Mentors) to build social awareness and leadership skills.
  • Build a bridge for dialogue between higher education and grassroot level school education.

OUR PROJECTS

Innovating rural education through Device Engineering Labs and peer video teaching initiatives.

Flagship Lab

Device Engineering Lab (DEL)

Objective

With the goal of developing the culture of ‘building from scratch’ and helping students think out of the box (beyond the textbook), Device Engineering Labs are set up in rural government schools.

  • Providing training on “making” things
  • Developing “scientific thinking”
  • Building the habit of hands-on learning
  • Designing and making of parts & working models using 3D Printer

Peer Learning

Teach in 10

Objective

Mentors from Higher Education Institutions mentor and train rural Government School students to develop and record 10-minute teaching videos on Mathematics, Science, Device Engineering, and 3D Printing.

  • Empowers rural students with presentation confidence
  • Encourages peer-to-peer video knowledge sharing
  • Covers core STEM & 3D printing concepts

OUR MISSION & VISION

We aim to strengthen the relationship between premier higher education institutions and grassroot level schools across India.

Core Vision Goals:

  • Strengthen the relationship between premier higher education institutions and rural government schools across India.
  • Develop the culture of 'Mentoring' in national education.
  • Make 'hands-on learning' an integral part of school education.
  • Upskill students from school level to make them competent and confident graduates in future.
